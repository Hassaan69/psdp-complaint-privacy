Privacy Policy

PSDP Complaint Portal
Planning & Development Department
Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. Introduction

The PSDP Complaint Portal (“App”) is operated by the Planning & Development Department, Government of Balochistan (“we”, “us”, “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and the choices available to you when you use the App.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

  • Identity details: full name, CNIC number, gender, district, qualification, and address.
  • Contact details: mobile number and email address.
  • Account credentials: password (stored securely by our backend systems) and authentication tokens on your device.
  • Complaint content: complaint subject, description, status history, and related departmental remarks.
  • Location data: GPS coordinates captured when you lodge a complaint, to verify the project site location.
  • Media: photographs taken with the camera or selected from your gallery as complaint evidence.
  • Device/session data: basic app session information needed to keep you logged in and secure the service.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your citizen account.
  • To authenticate you securely (including OTP verification where required).
  • To register, track, and resolve PSDP-related complaints.
  • To verify site evidence using photos and GPS coordinates.
  • To communicate updates related to your complaints or account.
  • To improve service quality, prevent misuse, and meet legal or audit obligations.

4. Permissions Used by the App

  • Camera: to capture complaint evidence photos.
  • Photo library: to attach existing photos when needed.
  • Location (while using the app): to record accurate site coordinates with a complaint.
  • Internet: to communicate with official P&D services.

These permissions are requested only for complaint and account features. You may deny permissions, but some features may not work without them.

5. Data Storage and Sharing

Your information is processed through official Government of Balochistan P&D digital services, including https://services.pnd.gob.pk. Data is used for public-service complaint handling within authorized government departments and monitoring staff.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only:

  • with authorized government officers handling your complaint;
  • when required by applicable law, regulation, court order, or audit;
  • with service providers strictly necessary to operate the platform, under confidentiality controls.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and complaint records for as long as needed to provide the service, meet departmental record-keeping requirements, and comply with applicable law. Evidence photos and GPS data linked to a complaint are retained as part of that complaint record.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including authenticated API access and controlled departmental access. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to safeguard your information.

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • You may review and update profile information available in the App.
  • You may request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • You may request account deletion / data removal subject to legal and departmental record obligations.
  • You may withdraw optional permissions from your device settings at any time.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for citizens submitting public development complaints and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when revisions are published. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.
