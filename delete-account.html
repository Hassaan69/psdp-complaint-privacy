How to delete your account

If you no longer want to use the PSDP Complaint Portal app, you can request deletion of your citizen account and associated personal data.

  1. Send an email to pnd.balochistan@gov.pk
  2. Use subject line: PSDP Complaint Portal Account Deletion Request
  3. Include:
    • Full name
    • Registered CNIC
    • Registered mobile number
    • Registered email address
    • A clear statement that you want your account deleted
  4. We will verify ownership of the account and process the request.
Contact email: pnd.balochistan@gov.pk

What data is deleted

After verification, we delete or anonymize the following account data where legally allowed:

  • Account profile details (name, CNIC, mobile, email, address, gender, district, qualification)
  • Login credentials and app authentication data
  • Personal contact information linked to the account

What data may be retained

Some complaint records, uploaded evidence photos, GPS site coordinates, and departmental remarks may be retained where required for government record-keeping, audit, legal, or public accountability purposes. Where retention is required, personal identifiers may be minimized or anonymized where possible.

Processing time

Account deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days after identity verification. You will receive confirmation on the email used for the request.
